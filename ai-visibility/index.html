Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to get on a call?

No. Everything happens by email: the snapshot, the questions, the onboarding, the reports. If you'd like a short video call, ask and we'll set one up, but you'll never need one.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO gets a page ranked in a list of links. Here, the goal is to be one of the few names an assistant recommends. The two overlap, but the assistants lean heavily on review sites, comparison lists and pages that answer buyer questions directly, and most SEO work doesn't measure AI answers at all.

How do you measure it?

We put each question to each assistant through its official API, with web search on where available: twice each in the free snapshot, three times on paid plans. Answers vary between runs and users, so we report percentages and print the method and date on every report.

Who writes the pages?

Drafted with AI, then checked by a person against a written quality list: accurate, specific to you, useful to a buyer, and fair to competitors. You approve every page before it goes live.

What do you need from me?

A short form after you sign up: your competitors, the questions buyers ask, and how you'd like pages published. Then a few minutes a month to approve pages.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. There are no contracts. Reply to any email from us and the next billing won't happen.
