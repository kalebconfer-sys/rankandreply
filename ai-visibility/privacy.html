Privacy: AI Visibility

Last updated October 12, 2026

In short

We use business contact details to send relevant business emails, and public information about companies to measure how AI assistants describe them. We don't sell personal information. Reply "no" to any email, or write to kaleb@rankandreply.com, and we'll stop contacting you and delete what we hold, except the record we need to keep you off our list.

This policy covers the AI Visibility service and our business outreach. Rank and Reply is a trade name of RevivaCatch LLC, Erie, Pennsylvania. Our Google Business Profile service has its own policy.

What we collect

  • Business contact details of people we email: name, work email, job title, company and company website. These come from commercial B2B data providers and from companies' own websites.
  • Public information about companies: the text of a company's public website, and what AI assistants say when asked questions about its category.
  • What clients give us: answers to our onboarding form, materials for pages, and access to publish on their website if they choose to grant it.
  • Emails you send us, including replies to our outreach.
  • Payment details are handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To send business emails we believe are relevant to your role, and to reply to you.
  • To measure how AI assistants answer buyer questions in a company's category, and to prepare reports, pages and recommendations.
  • To deliver the services clients buy, bill for them, and keep business records.

Who processes it for us

We use these providers to run the service. Each one processes data only to provide its service to us.

  • AI providers: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini) and Perplexity. They receive buyer questions, company names, public website text and, for clients, the materials needed for their pages. Replies you send us may be sorted and drafted with a model running on our own computer or with Anthropic's Claude. We use paid API access, not free consumer tiers, for any client or contact data.
  • Email and data tools: Google Workspace (email), Instantly (sending business email), Apollo (business contact data) and MillionVerifier (checking that email addresses are valid).
  • Payments and forms: Stripe (payments) and Tally (onboarding forms).

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Contact details of people who don't become clients: deleted within 12 months of our last email.
  • Opt-outs: we keep your email address on a suppression list indefinitely, only so we never email you again.
  • Client records: for the engagement plus the period needed for tax and accounting records.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop emails: reply "no" to any message, or use the unsubscribe link. We act on it the same business day.
  • See or delete your data: email kaleb@rankandreply.com. We'll respond within 30 days. If you live in a state with its own privacy law, you have the rights that law gives you, and we'll honor them.

Security

Data is stored in the accounts of the providers above and on our own computers, with access limited to the people who run the service.
