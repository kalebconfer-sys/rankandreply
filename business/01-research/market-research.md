# Market research and the decision

*Researched 2026-10-12 from a cold start: four research agents ran in parallel across freelance marketplaces, owner forums, business-model benchmarks and cold-email economics. Full reports with every source link are in [`sources/`](sources/). Figures marked (unverified) came from vendor marketing or self-reported claims and were not independently confirmed.*

## The question

Which business can a non-technical owner run mostly with AI, sell **by email only** (calls only with a buyer who has already said yes), start at about $5k/month within 90 days, and grow toward $100k/month?

## The answer

**An "AI search visibility" service for B2B companies.** We find out whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude recommend a company when its buyers ask for a recommendation, show the company exactly who gets named instead, and then fix it every month for a flat fee.

The free sample that opens every sale is a report generated by the same four AIs you already use. That means the AIs aren't only doing the work behind the scenes: their answers are what the client is paying to change.

## Why not the others (short version)

- **n8n/Make builds on Upwork.** Real volume (about 3,300 AI-automation posts in May 2026), but the median fixed budget is **$150**. Posts get 20–50+ proposals, and the AI category had the **lowest proposal reply rate on Upwork: 3.8%** ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/upwork/reports/2026/03/all-industries)). Fine as a side income; it can't carry the business.
- **AI receptionists, missed-call text-back, review-reply tools, generic chatbots.** Saturated. They're bundled into Jobber, GoHighLevel ($97) and Semrush Local ($30/location).
- **Back-office automation (insurance certificates, property-management reports, freight paperwork).** The pain is real, but each one means integrating with industry software, competing with VC-funded vendors (freight AI has raised $130M+), or handling compliance. That's the wrong fight for a solo founder.
- **Document extraction SaaS.** There's a documented solo business at $9–16k MRR, but it requires building and securing a financial-data app first. Our research found about 40% of 5,000 vibe-coded apps leaked private data. It's a later product, not a start.
- **Pre-built website previews.** No documented revenue, and the tactic is already sold as a $49/month tool.

## The honest verdict on Rank and Reply as it stands

Weekly Google posts plus review replies is a **$30–$99/month software feature** (Semrush Local $30, Merchynt Paige $99, GoHighLevel $97) or a $125–$400 done-for-you service. At **$800–$1,500**, the price matches full local SEO, not this scope. Contractors also rarely buy by email without a call. **Recommendation:** stop selling it as the main offer.

The brand still works. "Rank and Reply" reads naturally as *ranking in the AI's reply*, and the domain, site and email are already paid for. The new offer page lives at `/ai-visibility/` and leaves the contractor page untouched until you decide.

## Who we sell to first (the test)

The first 30 days split email volume between two segments. Reply data, not opinion, picks the winner by day 30.

| | Segment A (70% of volume) | Segment B (30% of volume) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | B2B software companies, 10–200 employees, US/UK/Canada | B2B service firms that buyers shortlist: managed IT / cybersecurity providers, and B2B agencies and consultancies |
| **Why** | Strongest evidence: G2's 50% stat is about software buyers. They have marketing budgets and are used to buying by email | Less AEO competition. Each won client is worth $2k–$10k+/month to them, so a $1,500 retainer is easy to justify |
| **Decision maker** | Founder/CEO under 50 people; VP or Head of Marketing above | Owner/CEO; marketing manager |
| **Buyer question we test** | "What's the best [category] software for [use case]?" | "Who are the best [service] providers for [type of company] in [region]?" |

## Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **We can't guarantee AI rankings.** Nobody controls what the AIs say | We sell deliverables (pages written, fixes shipped, listings submitted, monthly measurement), never guaranteed placement. We report honestly, including flat months. Same rule as the existing site: no results claims we can't back. |
| AI answers vary by run, user and location | We sample each question several times per engine, report percentages rather than single answers, and state the method and date on every report. |
| Google penalizes mass-produced AI content | 4–8 genuinely useful pages a month per client, each checked by a human against a written QA list. No mass page generation. |
| Competition is rising (AEO agencies, $29–$499 tracking tools) | Our wedge is done-for-you at the low end: $490 entry, $1,500/month, month-to-month, no calls, and four engines measured. Tools only measure; we fix. |
| Cold email deliverability | Separate sending domains, warmed inboxes, verified lists, low volume per inbox, no tracking pixels. Full playbook in `../04-outreach/cold-email.md`. |
| Founder time | AI does the measurement, the drafts and the reports. You approve and QA. Plan for about 2 hours a day in month 1 and about 3–4 hours per client per month after that, until a contract editor takes over QA. |

## Kill criteria (when to change course)

Decide these before starting, so the decision isn't emotional:

- **By day 30:** At least **3,000 emails** delivered with bounces under 2%, and **15+ people saying "yes, send it"** to the free report. If replies come in under 0.3% positive across both segments after two rewrites of the copy, the hook is wrong. Run a council debate on the reply data before sending more.
- **By day 60:** At least **2 paying clients** (either tier). If there are zero after about 40 free reports delivered, buyers want the report but not the service. Switch to selling the $490 audit as a standalone product and the tracking as a $99–$299/month self-serve tool, using the same engine.
- **By day 90:** **$5k+/month recurring.** If we're below that, the council reviews segment, price and offer, and picks one change for the next 30 days.
