# Source report: freelance-marketplace demand (2026-10-12)

Research agent report, kept as written. Upwork, Fiverr and Reddit pages couldn't be opened directly from the research environment, so these figures come from search-result snippets of those pages. Posting dates are approximate.

**Read Upwork budgets with care.** The Upwork minimum is $5 fixed / $3 per hour ([Upwork help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063418)), so many "$5 fixed" posts are placeholders, with the real price agreed in chat (inference, unverified).

## Market baseline
- **May 2026 volume:** Upwatcher counted **3,324 "ai automation" postings** ([upwatcher](https://www.upwatcher.io/market/ai-automation/)).
- **Rates:**
  - Median hourly $29.50 (P75 $40, P90 $60).
  - Median fixed budget $150 (n=1,266).
- **Skill tags:**
  - AI agent development: 682
  - n8n: 534
  - Chatbot development: 282
- **Demand growth:** The Upwork In-Demand Skills 2026 report shows AI integration +178% YoY and AI chatbot development +71% ([Upwork press](https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-in-demand-skills-2026-demand-for-top-ai-skills-more-than-doubles-as-ai-is-embedded-into-everyday-work)).
- **Headwinds:**
  - Upwork cut its 2026 revenue outlook to $730–750M, saying AI is automating lower-complexity work ([pymnts](https://pymnts.com/earnings/2026/upwork-navigates-cross-currents-as-ai-reshapes-freelance-demand)).
  - Earnings for "AI-based execution tasks" fell 28% YoY ([Upwork FWI](https://www.upwork.com/research/research-future-workforce-index-2026)).
- **Low reply rate on AI work:**
  - The AI category had the lowest proposal reply rate of any category in March 2026: **3.8%** ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/upwork/reports/2026/03/all-industries)).
  - The platform-wide reply rate is 7.45% ([GigRadar](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-market-report-2026)).

## Upwork mechanics
- **Connects:**
  - $0.15 each ([aiproposer](https://aiproposer.com/learn/upwork/upwork-connect-price-2026)).
  - 6–32 per proposal, depending on the source.
- **Freelancer fee:** 0–15% per contract ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free)).
- **Project Catalog:** Fixed-price packages that buyers purchase in a few clicks, with no proposal and no call ([Upwork](https://investors.upwork.com/news-releases/news-release-details/upworks-global-launch-project-catalogtm-offers-businesses-new)). Live examples:
  - [n8n/Make/Zapier workflow, $100–550](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/development-it-custom-ai-automation-workflow-using-n8n-make-or-zapier-2028961213176845111)
  - [AI voice receptionist, $85/150/450](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/admin-customer-support-ai-voice-agent-ai-calling-bot-n8n-gohighlevel-ai-receptionist-automation-2048898265303954896)
  - No public sales-volume data.
- **New freelancers:** Typically send 50–100 proposals before a first hire ([zenlance](https://zenlance.net/how-many-proposals-should-you-send-on-upwork/)). Unverified.
- **Off-platform:** Taking a client off-platform without the conversion fee is circumvention and can lead to suspension ([Upwork help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048105134)).
- **Proposals and AI:** Auto-submitting proposals with bots risks a ban. AI drafts that a human reviews and sends are tolerated ([giguphq](https://giguphq.com/blog/upwork-ai-proposals-banned-2026)). Unverified against the Upwork ToS text.

## Fiverr, Contra and others
- **Fiverr fees:** 20% seller commission.
- **Fiverr gigs:**
  - n8n gigs mostly have thin review counts ($5–45, 3–12 reviews). One shows 177 reviews at $25 ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/n8n-automation)).
  - Chatbot gigs: $95–300.
- **Fiverr demand:** "Claude Code specialist" demand up 938% in six months ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/news/business-trends-index-ai-2026)).
- **Contra:** 0% commission. n8n rates run $15–80/hr ([Contra](https://contra.com/hire/n8n-freelancers)).

## Agent's ranking
1. **Productized n8n/Make builds plus "fix my broken workflow" packages.** Highest volume and sellable with no call, but cheap and crowded.
2. **Doc-trained chatbots.** High volume; fixed packages; hosting brings recurring revenue.
3. **GHL plus AI voice/chat agents for white-label agencies.** Highest prices and the strongest retainer signals.
